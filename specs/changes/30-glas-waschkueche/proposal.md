# Proposal: glazed top metre in the Bad / Waschküche partition

## What

Issue #30: the partition between the Bad Nadine & Till and the Waschküche (the Bad's north wall, y 2.62 .. 2.79,
OG) gets an **Oberlicht**: a band of satin glass 1.00 m high directly under the 2.20 ceiling (sill 1.20), 10 cm
short of the side walls, in a pine frame lining the full wall depth, split by two mullions into three panes. The
north shower pane of the Bad ends on the first mullion. The Waschküche's four lamps over the table move down
under the sill.

## Why

The Bad has a window, the Waschküche none ("So kommt Licht vom Bad, dass ihr ein Fenster hat in die
Waschküche"). #33 and #29 kept the wall's top metre free on both sides for this.

## Scope

- In: the opening in the wall (house and the Elternbad sub-model), frame, mullions, beads, glass; the four
  Waschküche lamps lowered; tests; plans regenerated; README, CLAUDE.md.
- Out (non-goals): an openable sash; changes to the shower layout; new lights.

## Expected outcome

- Glass from OG + 1.20 (+ frame) to the ceiling, x 5.69 .. 8.49, in both the house and `web/elternbad.html`.
- No Part of either room (tiles, limestone, lamps, mirror, furniture) inside the glazing's box, except the
  north shower pane and its rail ending on the mullion at x 6.49.
- Still 31 OG Wandlampen; the four over the Waschküche table with their top under 1.20.
